A closet that builds itself.

Take one mirror photo a day. The app finds each piece of clothing, works out whether it has seen it before, and builds my closet from what I actually wear.

Photos never leave the device and faces are never stored.

How I'm building it

  1. Test the free on-device models on a phone first.
  2. Build the pipeline on sample data, with tests.
  3. Real models, a short inbox of yes or no questions, then install as an app.
